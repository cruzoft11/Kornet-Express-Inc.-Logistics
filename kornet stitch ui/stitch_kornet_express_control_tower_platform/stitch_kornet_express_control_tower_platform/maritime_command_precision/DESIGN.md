## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an industrial-grade, mission-critical logistics operations platform for Kornet Express Inc. Drawing on over two decades of multimodal freight-forwarding excellence across the Philippine archipelago and international trade lanes, the interface projects authoritative stability, operational vigilance, and razor-sharp data clarity.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modern** with **Technical Precision Control Tower** architecture:
- **Calm, Mission-Critical Tone:** The UI acts as a silent, high-efficiency instrument panel. It prevents operator fatigue during intense 12-hour shifts managing air, ocean, drayage, and customs brokerage workflows.
- **Extreme Density & Scannability:** White space is purposeful and metered rather than decorative. Information hierarchy is enforced through contrast, micro-typography, and crisp structural delineations.
- **Architectural Utility:** Inspired by port terminal operating systems, vessel radar readouts, and global customs manifests. It avoids trendy consumer app tropes in favor of structural clarity, instant legibility of alphanumeric tracking codes, and unyielding reliability.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unmistakable maritime logistics identity anchored by Kornet Navy, reinforced by specialized operational blues, and highlighted by high-contrast signals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#071D49` - Kornet Navy):** The structural core for navigation banners, primary actions, and commanding titles. Conveys sovereignty, institutional trust, and maritime pedigree.
- **Deep Navy (`#102B59`):** Secondary structural fill for active sidebar items, header cards, and table highlights.
- **Secondary (`#1769A8` - Operational Blue):** Interactive elements, linked waybills, progress states, and action triggers.
- **Accent Accent (`#2D86C5` - Bright Blue):** Focus states, live location pings, and active route segments.
- **Tertiary (`#C1121F` - Kornet Red):** Reserved for mission-critical alerts, expedited priority flags, and customs hold notifications.

### Neutrals & Surfaces (Light Mode Default)
- **Base Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` (Soft Surface) creates an anti-glare operational field.
- **Card & Data Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with `#DCE2EB` structural dividing borders.
- **Typography:** Primary text rests at `#111827` (near-black readability); auxiliary and structural labels rest at `#697386` (Muted Text) and `#28343D` (Graphite).

### Dark Mode Operations (Night Terminal & Port Operations)
- **Base Canvas:** `#061225`
- **Raised Panel Surface:** `#0B1B31`
- **Elevated Interactive Surface:** `#102B59`
- **Dark Structural Border:** `#263B5E`

### Functional Status Tokens
- **Success (`#087443`):** Customs cleared, gate-in confirmed, shipment delivered.
- **Warning (`#B7791F`):** Demurrage risk, transshipment delay, inspection scheduled.
- **Error / Hold (`#B42318`):** Seal discrepancy, customs hold, documentation rejection.

## Typography

The typography strategy separates natural human language from critical alphanumeric tracking parameters.

- **Primary Interface Typeface (Inter):** Applied across global controls, standard data labels, form labels, contextual help, and body copy. Selected for its neutral legibility, tall x-height, and robust visual weight separation.
- **Operational Data Typeface (Monospaced / JetBrains Mono fallback for IBM Plex Mono):** Strictly deployed for all reference values: Air Waybill (AWB), Ocean Bill of Lading (B/L), Container ISO codes (e.g., `MSKU0928341`), Customs Declaration numbers, Seal codes, Vehicle Identification Numbers (VIN), and GPS terminal positions. This eliminates character misinterpretation (e.g., zero vs. capital 'O', one vs. capital 'I') and ensures tabular alignment in manifest tables.
- **Casing Rules:** Table headers, status pills, and tracking code types (e.g., `BL#`, `CONT#`, `ETA`) must use uppercase styling with subtle positive letter spacing (`0.03em` to `0.05em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an enterprise fluid grid calibrated for dense desktop environments (1440px+ and 1920px terminal displays), with responsive contraction down to ruggedized tablet screens for port marshals and warehouse supervisors.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Control Tower Architecture:** Fixed-width operational left rail (collapsed at 64px, expanded at 240px), persistent global tracking search top bar (48px high), and multi-pane workspace layout (master-detail views, real-time split-screen vessel/truck telemetry).
- **Dense Spatial Scale:** Standard layout relies on a strict 4px base increment. Data tables use an ultra-compact row height (36px to 40px) with horizontal internal padding of `0.75rem` (`space-md`) to ensure dispatchers can view 20+ shipments per viewport without scrolling.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1280px - 1920px+):** 12-column grid, fluid gutters of `1rem`, persistent side panels for shipment timelines and live customs telemetry.
  - **Tablet Landscape (1024px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, drawer-based secondary details panel.
  - **Rugged Handheld / Mobile (Under 1024px):** Single-column stacked cards, sticky quick-action dispatch buttons, horizontal scroll data tables with pinned primary identifier columns.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects theatrical drop shadows in favor of **low-contrast outlines** and **tonal layering**. This ensures screen real estate stays crisp under bright warehouse lighting or darkened night-shift dispatch rooms.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#F8FAFC` (Light) / `#061225` (Dark). Completely flat background field.
- **Level 1 (Panels, Data Grids, Card Modules):** `#FFFFFF` (Light) / `#0B1B31` (Dark). Outlined with a 1px solid border (`#DCE2EB` in Light, `#263B5E` in Dark). No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus, Modals, Flyout Inspector Drawers):** Tonal surface with an ambient low-profile shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(7, 29, 73, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(7, 29, 73, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Alert Overlays, Urgent Dispatch Banners):** Framed with a 1px solid accent boundary (`#C1121F` or `#1769A8`) and backed with 4px directional structural cues.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry. 

- Base UI components (buttons, input fields, badges, table rows) leverage a disciplined `0.25rem` (4px) border radius.
- Larger structural panels, modals, and metric KPI cards use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Status indicator pills and live tracking beacon nodes use fully rounded pill shapes to distinguish transient operational states from permanent structural cards.
- Sharp, micro-radius corners emphasize engineering precision, software efficiency, and enterprise durability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#071D49` fill, white text, 4px corner radius, 36px standard height (`space-sm` vertical, `space-lg` horizontal padding). Transitions to `#102B59` on hover. Focus state exhibits a 2px offset ring in `#2D86C5`.
- **Operational / Secondary Action:** White background, 1px solid `#DCE2EB` border, text in `#28343D`. Hover shifts background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#1769A8`.
- **Critical / Danger Action:** `#C1121F` background with white text, reserved exclusively for irreversible actions (e.g., Hold Release Cancellation, Manifest Voiding).
- **Table Inline Actions:** 28px micro-buttons with monospaced icon-text pairings for instant scan-and-click operations.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Tracking Search Input:** 40px height, high-contrast `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#DCE2EB` border. Placeholder text in `#697386`. Left-aligned search icon with right-aligned keyboard shortcut badge (`Cmd+K`).
- **Data Table Filters:** 32px height, compact font sizing, instant dropdown badges. Active filters highlight with a `#1769A8` border and light blue tint (`#F0F7FD`).

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Structural Construction:** 20px height, uppercase `label-sm` font, monospaced identifier where applicable, 2px internal padding.
- **Customs / Shipment Status Types:**
  - *In Transit:* Tinted Operational Blue background (`rgba(23, 105, 168, 0.1)`), `#1769A8` text, pulsing 6px dot.
  - *Cleared / Delivered:* Tinted Success Green background (`rgba(8, 116, 67, 0.1)`), `#087443` text.
  - *Exception / Customs Hold:* Tinted Error Red background (`rgba(193, 18, 31, 0.1)`), `#C1121F` text.
  - *Pending Documents:* Tinted Amber background (`rgba(183, 121, 31, 0.1)`), `#B7791F` text.

### Manifest & Shipment Data Tables
- **Header Row:** 32px height, `#F8FAFC` background, 1px solid bottom border (`#DCE2EB`), uppercase 11px semi-bold text with sorting arrows.
- **Data Rows:** Alternating hover highlight (`#F0F7FD`), 38px height. Primary reference column (AWB/BOL) set in monospaced weight 500 font (`code-md`), directly linked in `#1769A8`.
- **Pinned Columns:** The left-most identification column remains sticky on horizontal viewport overflow.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Compact 16x16px square with 2px corner radius. `#071D49` fill with white checkmark when selected; `#DCE2EB` border on neutral state. High hit-area padding (8px transparent hit zone) to prevent mis-clicks during rapid manifest batch selections.

### Specialized Logistics Components
- **Milestone Route Stepper:** Horizontal tracking node diagram linking Origin CFS, Port of Loading (POL), Port of Discharge (POD), Customs Inspection, and Final Destination Delivery with color-coded completed segments (`#1769A8`), active animated segment (`#2D86C5`), and upcoming segments (`#DCE2EB`).
- **Telemetry Metric Cards:** White containers displaying gross weight (KG), volume (CBM), total TEU count, and customs SLA countdown timers with prominent numerical values and secondary monospaced unit identifiers.
